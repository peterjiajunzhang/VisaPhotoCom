QUESTIONS

Downloads, privacy and photo checks.

What does Visa Photo 1-Tap do?

It is a family of independently listed photo preparation apps. Pick the app for your destination to crop a portrait, replace the background and review photo information before a paid JPEG export.

Which apps can I download on Android?

The US app has both App Store and Google Play downloads here. Select Android above to find it. The other destination cards link to their iPhone listings and app websites.

Are my photos processed on my phone?

Portrait processing runs on your device, with no portrait upload to our servers. The app stores handle purchases. Android Google SDKs may send diagnostic data. Read the privacy policy for your app and platform on its website.

Where can I get support?

Open your country’s app website and choose Support. For the US app, visit US support, the iPhone policy or the Android policy.
