DV Lottery photo requirements: avoid the mistakes that disqualify entries

By the Visa Photo 1-Tap developer · Official sources checked November 14, 2026

In the Diversity Visa (DV) program the photo is not a formality. The Department of State’s instructions say you may be ineligible for a DV if the entry photographs for you and your family do not fully meet the specifications, have been manipulated, or were already used in a previous year’s entry. Unlike a visa application, there is no chance to fix it later.

What is the short answer?

Each person on the entry needs a new photo taken within the last six months: a square JPEG of exactly 600 × 600 pixels, 240 kB or smaller, in colour, full face, neutral expression, eyes open, no glasses, against a plain white or off-white background, with no digital manipulation. As of November 14, 2026, the Department of State has not published DV-2027 or DV-2028 registration dates; do not trust any site that claims otherwise.

Is DV-2028 registration open?

Not according to official sources checked on November 14, 2026. The Electronic Diversity Visa website (dvprogram.state.gov) says entries are not being accepted and still describes the DV-2026 period (October 2 to November 7, 2024). On November 5, 2025 the Department of State announced changes to the DV entry process and said it would announce the DV-2027 registration start date “as soon as practicable”. We found no official announcement of DV-2027 or DV-2028 registration dates on travel.state.gov or dvprogram.state.gov. We will not guess dates. When registration opens, it will be announced on those two official sites, and entries are only accepted on dvprogram.state.gov.

The most recent official instructions are for DV-2026. The photo rules below come from those instructions and the Department of State’s photo pages; check the new instructions when they are published, in case anything changes.

What are the official DV entry photo requirements?

FormatJPEG (.jpg)
PixelsSquare, 600 × 600 pixels
File sizeEqual to or less than 240 kB
Scanned photoIf scanning a print: 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm), scanned at 300 pixels per inch
Head size50%–69% of the image height, chin to top of head
RecencyTaken within the last six months
BackgroundPlain white or off-white
PoseIn colour and in focus, full face, directly facing the camera, neutral expression, both eyes open
Glasses and accessoriesNo eyeglasses; no headphones or wireless devices; hearing devices may be worn
ClothingEveryday clothing; no uniforms except religious clothing worn daily; no hat or head covering hiding the hair or hairline unless worn daily for religious purposes
Who needs a photoYou, your spouse and each derivative child on the entry

Sources: DV-2026 Program Instructions (“Submitting a Digital Photograph”) and the Department of State’s Photo Requirements page. Note that the DV pixel size is fixed at 600 × 600, unlike the DS-160, which accepts 600–1200. The instructions write the printed head size as “22 mm and 35 mm”, while the Photo Composition Template and photo FAQ say 25–35 mm; 1 inch is 25.4 mm.

Which photo mistakes can disqualify a DV entry?

  • Reusing last year’s photo. The instructions say submitting the same photograph that was submitted with a prior year’s entry will make you ineligible. Take a new photo for every year you enter.
  • A manipulated photo. The instructions warn that a photograph that has been manipulated may make you ineligible, and the Department of State says photos must not be digitally enhanced or altered to change your appearance.
  • A photo older than six months, or one that no longer looks like you.
  • Glasses, including clear prescription glasses.
  • Missing or wrong family photos. Your spouse and each eligible child need their own photo meeting the same standard. A photo of the wrong person is a serious problem.
  • A non-white background, shadows, or a busy room behind you.
  • Wrong pixel size or format — for example a 1200 × 1200 DS-160 file, a rectangle, or a HEIC or PNG file renamed to .jpg.
  • Copies of other photos — photos copied from driver’s licences or other documents, snapshots, magazine photos or full-length photos are not acceptable.

How do I take a DV photo that does not need editing?

Because a manipulated photo can make you ineligible, the lowest-risk approach is a photo that needs no editing beyond cropping and resizing:

  1. Stand a step in front of a real plain white or off-white wall, or hang a flat white sheet.
  2. Face a window with soft daylight so there are no shadows on your face or the wall.
  3. Ask someone to take the photo with the main rear camera at your eye level, at least 4 feet (1.2 m) away. Turn off filters, beauty modes and portrait mode.
  4. Remove glasses, earbuds and hats; look straight at the camera with a neutral expression and both eyes open.
  5. Crop the original to a square with your head at 50%–69% of the height, resize to exactly 600 × 600 pixels and save as a JPEG of 240 kB or less. The Department of State’s photo pages list a free photo tool that crops to exactly 600 × 600 pixels; it notes that it is for cropping only and does not check quality.

How do I check the file before submitting?

  • Dimensions: exactly 600 × 600. On an iPhone, open the photo in Photos and swipe up; on a Mac, Preview › Tools › Show Inspector; on Windows, Properties › Details.
  • Format: a real JPEG, not a renamed HEIC or PNG.
  • Size: 240 kB or less. Staying at or below 240,000 bytes satisfies either way of counting a kilobyte. A 600 × 600 photo at a high quality setting is usually well under the limit.
  • Content: view at full size and compare with the official photo examples — focus, shadows, background, head size and expression.
  • Every person: repeat the checks for your spouse’s and children’s photos.

After you submit, the instructions say you will see a confirmation screen with your name and a unique confirmation number. Print it and keep the number; you need it to check your status.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a DV Lottery photo?

A square JPEG of 600 × 600 pixels, no larger than 240 kB. A scanned print must be 2 × 2 inches scanned at 300 pixels per inch.

Can I use the same photo as last year?

No. The DV instructions say submitting the same photograph as a prior year’s entry will make you ineligible. Take a new photo each year.

When does DV-2028 registration open?

As of November 14, 2026, the Department of State had not published DV-2027 or DV-2028 registration dates. Check travel.state.gov and dvprogram.state.gov; entries are only accepted on dvprogram.state.gov.

Can I wear glasses in a DV Lottery photo?

No. The DV instructions say do not wear eyeglasses.
