# China visa photos for applicants in the United States

The English and Simplified Chinese upload guides now help readers find the U.S. visa office that serves their place of residence, open China Online Visa Application and identify the photo and passport handoff steps. The country site's U.S. link opens that section in the existing guide. The separate Australia route remains available from both sites. [Chinese Embassy jurisdiction notice](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/zytz/202406/t20240629_11444432.htm) · [Visa application FAQ](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/lsfw/zj/qz2021/202309/t20230919_11144908.htm)

Los Angeles's published procedure waives the paper photo after a successful digital upload. The Washington and New York guides describe when to proceed and bring a paper photo after repeated upload failures. Both language versions point to the receiving office's own instructions and explain the separate JPEG and paper dimensions. [Los Angeles ordinary visa procedure](https://losangeles.china-consulate.gov.cn/eng/visa/chinavisa/202509/t20250920_11712348.htm) · [Washington photo upload guidance](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/lsfw/zj/qz2021/202509/t20250920_11712388.htm) · [New York online application guidance](https://newyork.china-consulate.gov.cn/eng/tzgg/202509/t20250924_11714475.htm)

Pre-publication Chrome checks used 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px in each language. Five tasks per language and viewport matched Texas to Washington, D.C., Ohio to New York, Nebraska to Chicago, Washington state to San Francisco and Southern California to Los Angeles. The tests also checked the homepage and intra-page links, the links to the Australian guide, local assets, horizontal overflow, language metadata and the notices before the store button. The shortcut below the quick answer reaches the iPhone preparation section in both languages, with the heading visible below the sticky header at 320 and 390 px. The 14-page guide generator check passed. Twelve public endpoints returned HTTP 200, including the official portal, office instructions and the U.S. and Australian App Store listings.

The desktop QR uses the same local PNG as the Australian guide. An independent decoder recovered the same full campaign URL from the PNG and the code as rendered in the desktop browser. The regular App Store badge is available on phone, tablet and desktop.
